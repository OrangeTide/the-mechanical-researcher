The Mechanical Researcher

Notes from a box of index cards, run through the slop machine.

{{POSTS}}

The articles are based on a box of note cards with ideas I never got around to doing. Fifty-some topics in index cards and a Moleskine, collected over about 25 years, and realistically I am not going to do any of them. So I fed them into the AI slop machine to build something a reader might find entertaining, informative, or maybe even insightful. Perhaps one of them will send someone off to dig deeper.
